Your privacy.

FAST is Jacob Cloete’s adventure project. If you join an interest list, Jacob uses your name, email and chosen trip to contact you about that adventure, including its dates, price and preparation.

What is saved

Your name, email, trip, signup time, contact permission and interest status are saved in private storage on the Hostinger server. Signups are not public, are not sold and are not added to an unrelated newsletter. No payment, medical information or passport details are requested.

A scrambled identifier based on your IP address is used for abuse prevention, for up to an hour. The signup database does not retain your raw IP address. Hostinger and the services that load images, video and optional maps may keep their own technical access logs.

Your control

Joining is optional. After a new signup, you receive a private link on screen that lets you delete that signup and its contact details. Save it; no confirmation email is currently sent. A repeat signup keeps the existing record and does not issue a replacement private link.

You can also reply to any trip email from Jacob to withdraw your interest, ask for a copy of your details or correct them. Records expire four months after the first day of the proposed departure month and are cleared when the signup service next runs. Earlier deletion is available through your private link.

Images and maps

The homepage loads stock imagery and video from Pexels. The world map is a local file. Opening a 3D preview loads MapLibre from jsDelivr, satellite imagery from Esri and terrain from AWS. These providers receive technical connection information. The site does not use advertising trackers or set analytics cookies.

An interest list, not a booking

The trips, training framework and four-guest group size are plans. Final dates, price, availability, suitability and arrangements must be agreed separately before a place is confirmed. No payment is taken here.
