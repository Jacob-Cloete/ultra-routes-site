12 weeks before departure

Training &
equipment.

Personalised training, weekly video calls and a trip-specific kit list. The pace on the trip combines running and hiking.

  1. Weeks 1–4

    Base fitness

    Assess your starting fitness, establish a training routine and choose your equipment.

  2. Weeks 5–8

    Hills & pack practice

    Practise climbing, descending and moving with your loaded pack.

  3. Weeks 9–10

    Consecutive trail days

    Test your food and equipment over back-to-back days.

  4. Weeks 11–12

    Taper & final checks

    Reduce training volume and finalise equipment and travel arrangements.

A proposed preparation framework, adapted to your experience. It builds on an existing running or hiking base; it isn’t a promise of readiness in 12 weeks.
